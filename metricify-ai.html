Founded in 2024. Focused on the next decision.

Metricify AI was founded in 2024 with a focus on commerce intelligence. Its platform brings demand forecasting, campaign measurement and procurement workflows into a connected view of the business.

The problem is familiar to teams selling products: attention changes quickly, campaign activity is spread across channels, and supplier decisions affect whether inventory arrives when it is needed. Understanding one part of that picture is useful. Connecting the parts is where Metricify AI focuses its product.

Its message—“See the signal. Make the move.”—captures that approach. The goal is to help teams move from scattered information to a clearer decision, with business rules and human approval boundaries shaping what happens next.

Four connected solutions.

Metricify AI’s published platform overview describes four areas of work:

  • Demand forecasting. The company combines social, search, marketplace and sales signals to help teams recognise changes in demand and connect forecasts to inventory planning.
  • Campaign intelligence. Tools focus on understanding campaign impact across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and creator activity, bringing attention and business outcomes into the same conversation.
  • Autonomous procurement agents. AI agents are designed to support supplier negotiations on pricing and service levels within defined policies, budgets and approval boundaries.
  • Supply risk and resilience. The platform is intended to surface bottlenecks, delays and supplier risks so teams can include them in procurement and inventory decisions.

These are related business questions. A change in demand can affect an inventory plan. Campaign activity can change where a team expects demand to appear. Supplier terms and delivery windows can affect whether the business is ready to respond.

One workflow, several teams.

Consider a commerce team investigating a product that is gaining attention. Marketing wants to understand which channels are contributing. Planning needs to evaluate whether the interest could turn into sustained demand. Procurement needs to consider supplier offers, lead times and the limits the business has set.

This example illustrates the workflow Metricify AI is designed around; it is not a claim about a particular customer result. Bringing those questions together gives a prospective user a practical way to explore the product with their own data and decision process.

The company’s approach is relevant to teams working in commerce analytics, marketing measurement, inventory planning and procurement. A useful starting point is a specific decision the team wants to improve, followed by a discussion of the data, integrations and review steps it requires.

Explore the product demo.

The Metricify AI website includes an interactive product demo with demand, campaign and negotiation views. Visitors can explore a demand chart, review an example campaign mix and replay a supplier-negotiation workflow. The negotiation view shows how an offer can be evaluated against pricing and service-level requirements before a counteroffer is prepared for approval.

These demonstrations introduce the product experience. Their example figures are not independently verified customer outcomes, live market quotes or a guarantee of future performance. Teams should evaluate product fit and data requirements for their own use case.
