Privacy & advertising.

Last updated November 8, 2026.

Who operates this site

Startup Bulletin is published by the Metricify AI team. For privacy questions, contact info@metricifyai.com.

Reading the site & hosting

This is a public, static publication hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not require a reader account or provide a newsletter signup form. Your browser sends technical information such as your IP address and requested page to the hosting provider when you visit. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Google AdSense & advertising

We use Google AdSense site code to connect this website to Google’s advertising service. Ad display depends on Google’s approval and our advertising settings. When advertisements are served, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google’s advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to show ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. Other advertising vendors may also use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver and measure advertisements. Information involved may include browser and device details, IP address, ad interactions and cookie identifiers, subject to the applicable consent choices and service settings.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read Google’s privacy policy.

Your advertising & cookie choices

You can manage or opt out of personalized Google advertising through My Ad Center. You can also visit aboutads.info/choices to learn about participating third-party vendors and available opt-out tools. Opting out of personalization does not necessarily remove all advertising.

Where a consent message is displayed, use its options to accept, refuse or manage advertising purposes and vendors. You can also control cookies in your browser settings; blocking or deleting cookies may affect how preferences are remembered. Availability and scope of controls depend on your region, browser and advertising provider.

Links, contact & retention

Articles link to other websites, including Metricify AI and source publications. Those websites have their own privacy practices. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information. Advertising and hosting providers determine retention for data they process under their own policies; this publication does not operate its own reader-account database.

Changes

We may update this page as the site’s services change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
